# Privacy Policy

Project GetBack runs locally on the user's machine. It reads locally stored data
(saved browser passwords, cookies, Wi-Fi profiles, and Discord authentication
tokens) and sends a summary to a Discord webhook configured by the operator.

- No data is sent anywhere except the configured webhook.
- No accounts, analytics, or telemetry.
- All processing happens on the local machine.
- The report is also saved locally to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\tmp_stealer\report.json`.
